About HP Newsletter

HP Newsletter is a student-built news website created as part of our internship work and college submission. The name HP comes from our names, Hemapriya and Pavithra, and the site brings category-wise articles, search, filters, featured stories, and a simple publishing workflow into one clean web experience.
